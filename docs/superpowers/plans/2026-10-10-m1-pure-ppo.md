# M1 pure PPO training implementation plan

> Execute inline with executing-plans, test-driven-development and verification-before-completion.

**Goal:** Train M1 with PPO-owned actions, planner-free rewards and real obstacle metrics.
**Architecture:** Keep the 16-action M1 PD interface, policy observations and strict encounter tracker. Remove MPC manager attachment, action takeover and imitation from the M1 training route. Preserve historical diagnostics, but do not run or restore WBC changes as prerequisites.
**Tech stack:** Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, project RSL-RL PPO, PyTorch.

## User-approved scope

User explicitly requests removing MPC and changing only reward/terrain for PPO exploration. No WBC, IK, servo, torque-limit or prescribed foot-trajectory changes. Mixed terrain and flat density x1.5 remain; initial reward changes address measured exploration obstacles, not relaxed success criteria. Short training health verification precedes long training; learned crossing is evaluated during training, not required of the initial random policy.
