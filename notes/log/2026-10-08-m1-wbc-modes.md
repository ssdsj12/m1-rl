## Scope and contract

CPU QP gains optional separation_matrix/separation_lower hard inequalities on
generalized acceleration. Included in every hierarchy stage and independent
post-solve residual check; defaultempty preserves previous equality contract.
Invalid/nonfinite dimensions reject. No actuator/default mode changes.

Candidate adapter takes boolean attached mask, point Jacobians/frames, known
evaluated acceleration bias, forcecapacity and explicit normalacc lowerbound.
Attached points use zero evaluated derivative equality. Released points use
normal acceleration inequality and normalforcemax0. Existing friction cone then
sets tangential force0 as well. Original slots and leverarms retained.
No complementarity relaxation permitting force on a released point.

Caller MUST provide physically valid modes and lowerbounds from current gap,
velocity, geometry and contact state. This function is NOT an estimator or
automatic release selector. No missing-state fallback or patch-ID differentiation.
