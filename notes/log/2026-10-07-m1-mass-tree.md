## Procedure and result

Run amp Python with PYTHONPATH pointing to installed omni.usd.libs and
LD_LIBRARY_PATH to that package's bin and bin/deps. No installation required.
17 rigid bodies, 16 revolute joints, one source fixed root joint (disabled by
the existing runtime floating-base adapter). Total authored mass
41.04531955718994 kg agrees with previously recorded native total mass.
BASE_LINK mass20.25 kg, COM(0.002176,0.011832,-0.009523)m is not centered.
Each leg mass5.198829889 kg, roughly12.67% of total: lifted-leg mass cannot
be omitted from predictive whole-body COM.
All child joint anchors zero. Axes ABAD=X, HIP/KNEE/FOOT=Y.
FBL_HIP parent quaternion is (1,0.000087265,0,0), not exactly identity;
do not silently assume all four chains identical/mirror-perfect.
